# Ressources
Implementing a computer algebra system doesn't come out of nowhere, even if certain parts or algorithms may have come from the authors themselves.

Here are listed various resources (web articles, books, papers, etc.) that have helped or may have helped the authors or that are simply of general interest.

> [!NOTE]
> The authors of Liyh have obviously not read every resource listed in detail, but these are part of their personal library on the subject, if this is of interest to others.
>
> There are many other elements that have helped to create certain parts of Liyh, such as exchanges, questions and answers, web articles, and even classic algebra textbooks.

## General
Resources on the subject in general.

 - **Winkler, F., Gonnet, G. H., Gruntz, D., & Bernardin, L.** (2003). [*Computer Algebra*](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/261521795_Computer_Algebra) – presents the principles of CAS and compares them with purely numerical approaches.

 - **von zur Gathen, J., & Gerhard, J.** (2013). [*Modern Computer Algebra*](https://doi.org/10.1017/CBO9781139856065) – takes a comprehensive look at the subject, based on the contributions of various historical authors.

 - **Geddes, K. O., Czapor, S. R., & Labahn, G.** (1992). [*Algorithms for Computer Algebra*](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/b102438) – the first comprehensive textbook to be published on the topic of computational symbolic mathematics.

 - **Cohen, J. S.** (2002). [*Computer Algebra and Symbolic Computation: Elementary Algorithms*](https://doi.org/10.1201/9781439863695) – provides a systematic approach for the algorithmic formulation and implementation of mathematical operations in computer algebra.

 - **Cohen, J. S.** (2003). [*Computer Algebra and Symbolic Computation: Mathematical Methods*](https://doi.org/10.1201/9781439863701) – provides a systematic approach for the algorithmic formulation and implementation of mathematical operations in computer algebra.

There are many others. The two books by Joel S. Cohen are probably the most accessible.

## Equations

 - **Petkovsek, M., Wilf, H., & Zeilberger, D.** [*A=B*](https://www2.math.upenn.edu/~wilf/AeqB.html) – "A=B" is about identities in general, and hypergeometric identities in particular, with emphasis on computer methods of discovery and proof.

 - **Sterling, L., Bundy, A., Byrd, L., O'Keefe, R., & Silver, B.** (1989). [*Solving symbolic equations with PRESS*](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0747-7171(89)80007-0) – describe a program, PRESS, (PRolog Equation Solving System) for solving symbolic, transcendental, non-differential equations in one or more variables.

 - **Bartlett, P.** (2013). [*Finding All the Roots: Sturm’s Theorem*](https://web.math.ucsb.edu/~padraic/mathcamp_2013/root_find_alg/Mathcamp_2013_Root-Finding_Algorithms_Day_2.pdf) – presents Sturm chains and an algorithm for finding all the roots of a polynomial.

 - **Sterling, L., Bundy, A., Byrd, L., O'Keefe, R., & Silver, B.** (1989). [*Solving symbolic equations with PRESS*](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0747-7171(89)80007-0) – Present a program (PRESS - PRolog Equation Solving System) for solving symbolic, transcendental, non-differential equations in one or more variables.

## Polynomials
### Factorization (mainly finite field)

 - **Jordan, D. E., Clapp, L. C., & Kain, R. Y.** (1967). [*Symbolic factoring of polynomials in several variables*](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/365758.365809) – An algorithm for finding the symbolic factors of a multivariate polynomial with integer coefficients is presented.

 - **Combs, W.** (2016). [*General Factoring Algorithms for Polynomials over Finite Fields*](https://encompass.eku.edu/etd/249/) – Algorithms for factoring polynomials with coefficients in finite fields.

 - **Wang, P. S.** (1978). [*An improved multivariate polynomial factoring algorithm*](https://www.ams.org/journals/mcom/1978-32-144/S0025-5718-1978-0568284-3/) – A new algorithm for factoring multivariate polynomials over the integers based on an algorithm by Wang and Rothschild is described.

 - **Wikipedia.** (n.d.). [*Factorization of polynomials*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Factorization_of_polynomials) – Describes some of the school methods used in Liyh.


### Gröbner basis

 - **Faugère, J.-C.** (2002). [*Calcul efficace des bases de Gröbner et Applications*](https://www-polsys.lip6.fr/~jcf/Papers/hdr_faugere.pdf) – Presents the general problem, standard algorithms, and modern Faugère algorithms (F4 and F5).

 - **Marcotte, L.** (2005). [*Les bases de Groebner et les ordres monomiaux*](https://archipel.uqam.ca/1309/) – Memoir presenting Gröbner basis, applications, and standard algorithms.

 - **Cox, D. A., Little, J., & O’Shea, D.** (2015). [*Ideals, Varieties, and Algorithms*](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-319-16721-3) – Textbook covering topics in algebraic geometry and commutative algebra with a strong perspective toward practical and computational aspects.


### Resultants

 - **Stiller, P. F.** (1996). [*An Introduction to the Theory of Resultants*](https://isc.tamu.edu/resources/preprints/1996/1996-02.pdf) – A "working man's" introduction to resultants.


## General calculus
### Limits

 - **Geddes, K. O., & Gonnet, G. H.** (1992). [*A New Algorithm for Computing Symbolic Limits Using Hierarchical Series*](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/221564331_A_New_Algorithm_for_Computing_Symbolic_Limits_Using_Hierarchical_Series) – Describe an algorithm for computing symbolic limits using hierarchical series.

 - **Gonnet, G. H., & Gruntz, D.** (1996). [*Limit Computation In Computer Algebra*](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/262178436_Limit_Computation_In_Computer_Algebra) – Present the Gruntz's algorithm for limit computation of exp-log functions.

### Integration

 - **Bronstein, M.** (1997). [*Symbolic Integration Tutorial*](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/2573905_Symbolic_Integration_Tutorial) – Introduces the problem and standard algorithms in computer algebra.

 - **Terelius, B.** (2006). [*Symbolic Integration*](https://www.academia.edu/26148447/BjornT_Symbolic_Integration) – Master's thesis on symbolic integration and presentation of standard algorithms in computer algebra.

## Interpolation

 - **Gavin, H. P.** (2011). [*The Levenberg-Marquardt method for nonlinear least squares curve-fitting problems*](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/The-Levenberg-Marquardt-method-for-nonlinear-least-Gavin/1d83aa91810162891c9f04e9a62da69197c78602) – Present the Levenberg-Marquardt curve-fitting method.

 - **Kaltenbach, M.** (2020). [*The Levenberg-Marquardt Method and its Implementation in Python*](https://kops.uni-konstanz.de/entities/publication/9197ed25-a358-4fd9-943f-1897414eb6df) – Master's thesis on the Levenberg-Marquardt algorithm.

## Simplifications

 - **Bailey, D. H., Borwein, J. M., & Kaiser, A. D.** (2013). [*Automated simplification of large symbolic expressions*](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S074771711300117X?via%3Dihub) – Present a set of algorithms for automated simplification of symbolic constants with rational and complex.

 - **Jeffrey, D. J., & Rich, A. D.** (1996). [*Simplifying Square Roots of Square Roots by Denesting*](https://cybertester.com/data/denest.pdf) – Introduces a denesting method.

 - **Carette, J.** (2004). [*Understanding expression simplification*](https://dl.acm.org/doi/abs/10.1145/1005285.1005298) – Give the first formal definition of the concept of simplification for general expressions in the context of Computer Algebra Systems.
